- `src/main/java/com/github/timan1802/typingbongocat/` and `src/main/resources/img/cat/`: from
  [timan1802/typing-bongo-cat](https://github.com/timan1802/typing-bongo-cat) at commit `4bcb81b`, listed as MIT on the

  the JetBrains Marketplace. It is not included in this repository or its releases; only the SHA-256 of release 1.16 is
  recorded.
- Ad-host rules are downloaded at runtime from the
  [AdGuard DNS filter](https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdguardSDNSFilter) and are not part of this repository.
